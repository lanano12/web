How ready are you for the Cyber Resilience Act?

Twelve quick questions, about three minutes. You’ll get an indicative readiness score and your biggest gaps — starting with vulnerability-reporting readiness.

Runs entirely in your browserYour answers stay in this pageNot legal advice
00Do you sell or supply software, or products containing software, to customers in the EU?
01Can outside researchers and customers easily report a vulnerability to you (e.g. a security.txt file or a published security contact)? Reporting priority
02Could you triage a reported, actively exploited vulnerability and make a decision within 24 hours? Reporting priority
03Do you know who would submit an early warning through ENISA’s single reporting platform, and how? Reporting priority
04Do your builds generate a machine-readable software bill of materials (SBOM)?
05Do you check releases for known exploitable vulnerabilities before shipping?
06Have your main products been threat-modelled, with the decisions recorded?
07Do your products ship with secure defaults (no default passwords, minimal exposed services)?
08Have you defined a support period and a way to ship security updates for each product?
09Do you publish a coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy?
10Do you keep technical documentation of your security design and risk assessment?
11Do you know whether any of your products are “important” or “critical” under the CRA?
