Before we start

A few
useful answers.

Can you work with an existing product?

Yes. Existing firmware, established hardware and difficult-to-reproduce faults are part of embedded engineering. We start by understanding the current behaviour and the constraints on changing it.

Can you help with CRA readiness?

On the engineering side: product risk assessment, vulnerability handling, SBOMs, verification and technical evidence. Legal interpretation and formal conformity decisions belong with your legal advisers and the appropriate assessment route.

How does an engagement start?

A conversation about the product, the problem and what you already know. From there we agree a useful scope, the deliverables and how success will be checked.

Who owns the work?

Your code, build instructions, engineering decisions and documentation stay in your repositories. Handover is part of the work.
