[Verified] Below is what the Tate website itself confirms about Impressionist art currently on display at Tate Modern in London—together with the exact works and where to find them inside the building.

Impressionist artists on display at Tate Modern

Claude Monet

  • Work: Water‑Lilies (Nymphéas), 1907.
  • Status / where on Tate’s site: Listed as a free display: “Claude Monet” at Tate Modern (the display page features the painting). The page does not specify a room number; Tate’s free collection displays are spread across the Natalie Bell Building (the original Boiler House) on Levels 2 & 4. Use the display page for the latest placement on the day. (Tate)

Edgar Degas

  • Work: Little Dancer Aged Fourteen, 1880–81, cast c.1922.
  • Location: On display at Tate Modern as part of the Media Networks collection display → Natalie Bell Building, Level 4 (East). (Tate)

Camille Pissarro

  • Work: Portrait of Félix Pissarro, 1881.
  • Location: On display at Tate Modern as part of Media Networks → Natalie Bell Building, Level 4 (East). (Tate)

Photos / URLs: The titles above are linked on the Tate site; click the citations to open Tate’s pages with images of the works (and the Monet display page). The thumbnail at the top is from Tate’s Monet display page. (Tate)

Helpful floor context (from Tate’s site)

Tate Modern’s free collection displays are organised across these signed areas in the Natalie Bell Building:

  • Level 2 East – “In the Studio.” (Tate)
  • Level 2 West – “Artist and Society.” (Tate)
  • Level 4 East – “Media Networks.” (where the Degas and Camille Pissarro works above are placed). (Tate)
  • Level 4 West – “Materials and Objects.” (Tate)

Use the lift/escalators from the Turbine Hall, then follow these section names on the wayfinding signs.

Notes on other Impressionists in Tate’s collection

These are in Tate’s holdings but not currently showing at Tate Modern per their artwork pages:

  • Alfred Sisley, The Small Meadows in Spring, By c.1881 – Not on display. (Tate)
  • Berthe Morisot, Girl on a Divan, c.1885 – currently noted as On loan (National Gallery Bicentenary) on Tate’s site. (Tate)

Quick checklist for your visit

  • Monet: open the Claude Monet display page before you go for the freshest placement (Tate rotates hangings; the page is the authority). (Tate)
  • Degas & Camille Pissarro: head to Media Networks on Level 4 (East) in the Natalie Bell Building; look for the Degas Little Dancer and Pissarro Portrait of Félix Pissarro in that section. (Tate)
