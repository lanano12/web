02 · What you would actually be doing

Shoot on the track · cut it the same night

Eight units, two years, 100% coursework. Units 1–7 are pass / fail. Unit 8 is the whole grade. Most weeks: shoot or edit something, write a short log of why you did it that way, hand it in on a blog or Padlet. No June exam about a TV drama someone else picked.

Year 12 · 2026–27

Learn the kit on real sessions. First films at the new standard after sub-16. Audio test. A short piece on how young runners get shown. Build the look of the channel.

On the track: live in the 15s.

Year 13 · 2027–28

Audio + visual + hub as proper pieces. Then Unit 8: one planned season of films that is the certificate.

On the track: break 15. Hunt 14.

What a normal week looks like

  • College sessions on cameras, Premiere, sound, a set brief.
  • You shoot around training — not instead of it.
  • A short journal after: what you tried, what failed, what you change next time.
  • Deadlines keep coming. Treat them like sessions. Missing one is a dropped workout.

Teachers still set some briefs in Year 12 (an advert, a podcast, a website). Your move is to answer those briefs with running wherever they let you — and keep a side folder of channel work so Unit 8 is already warm.

04 · Units 1–4 — portfolio ideas

Unit 1 starts here: film the session you already do

These are the learn-the-tools units. Pass / fail. Use them to get the channel standing.

Unit 1 · Skills. Film one easy run and one session on the same day. Test: phone vs college camera, mic on the move, Premiere cuts, captions for splits. Hand in the two clips + a page of what broke.
Unit 2 · Research. Pull apart two films: one Nick Bester race-POV, one Olympic training-block (Young / Sesemann / Heymans). Shot list. Who it is for. Then write a one-page treatment for your version of the same form.
Unit 3 · Professional practice. Build the one-page media kit. How Bester and other athlete-creators work with brands. Under-18 rules (parent on the email). A fake “if a local shop asked tomorrow” reply. Call sheet for a parkrun shoot. Card-show / eBay brain goes here: listing, value, showing up.
Unit 4 · Contextual. Short video essay: how young runners get shown vs how adults get shown. Parkrun vs elite-wave. Who is missing. Football-to-running switch as the case study — three minutes, not a memoir.

If the teacher sets a locked brief (an advert for a made-up brand), answer it with running kit / a youth-race / a club night. Ask first. If they say no, do their brief properly and keep your running version in the side folder.

05 · Units 5–7 — the three making blocks

5 · Audio

A. FPL-style weekly show, running subject. Same three chapters: last race / session · what changes · next target. 8–12 minutes.

B. Interview a senior club runner who already owns 15 or 14. You as host. Clean sound.

C. Race-week voice note as commentary over silent race footage.

6 · Visual

A. Full key session. Warm-up to last rep. Splits on screen. No talking-head until the end.

B. Race-POV of a target 5k. Bester structure: start, splits, last 400, debrief.

C. 90-second origin: football boots on a peg, first parkrun, 16:01 board.

7 · Hub

One page: films, official times (16:15 / 16:05 / 16:01 / next), link to Strava, next race.

Not a video game. Not an eBay shop. This is the platform the course asks for.

A clean hub beats a half-built game every time.

These feed Unit 8. Do not waste them on a topic you will never use again if you can help it.

06 · Unit 8 — championship project ideas

Unit 8 is the whole diploma grade. Ask the college to accept the topic early.

About 350 words of plan, the films, a journal, a debrief against the plan.

Main idea · Road to 15. Three to five films across Year 13: winter base at the new standard · one key session · race week + target 5k · debrief against 15:00 and the first look at 14. Hub holds them. Club screening as evaluation.
Alt · The 60 seconds. Same form, tighter promise: one season about what the next minute actually costs after 16:01. Good if a teacher wants a smaller brief.
Alt · Two jobs. Athlete + creator. One film on the session, one on the edit / media kit / how a junior asks for kit without looking fake. Still running at the centre.

What you hand in

  1. Proposal: why this audience, what films, how you will judge them.
  2. The work itself.
  3. Journal — decisions, not a diary of feelings.
  4. Evaluation: did club runners say it was honest; did the race film look built; did you hit the dates. Name what failed. A missed 15 still counts if the debrief is straight.

Do not write “Olympic standard 2028” as the Unit 8 outcome. That is the north star. The project is the next two standards on camera.

07 · How FPL, Strava, cards and football get in

Football is the origin · FPL is the show format · cards are the pitch brain · Strava is the clock

They make the running brand sharper. They do not get their own channel on this course.

Interest Steal this Put it here Leave it out of
FPL YouTubers Same show every week. Review / changes / next pick. Graphics. Deadline energy. Unit 5 audio series. Thumbnail and title practice. Unit 8 title. You are not an FPL tipster.
Strava He already does this. Public clock, photos, titles with flair — not a silent GPS dump. That is the brand voice. Unit 7 hub. End cards. Same captions on the films. Link: athletes/174172689 A screenshot dump with no film. Or a new “serious” voice that kills the flair.
Cards / eBay / shows What something is worth. Listing. Showing up in a room. Unit 3 media kit and “how I’d pitch a shop.” A trading-card mini-doc as the main project.
Football Origin. Team brain. Knowing a crowd. 90-second origin cut. Maybe matchday vs race-day. Equal billing with the 5k.

Sponsorship — later, not the first video

UK brands already pay runners who can make films. First deals at 16 look like kit and a code, with a parent on the email. The coursework builds the folder those emails need: films, times, a clean look, a one-page kit. Do the work first. The deal follows the folder.

08 · Films you can make before September

None of this needs the college kit. Phone + a cheap mic + a free edit app.

You walk in with proof you already work like this.

  1. Origin 90. Football stopped. First parkrun. 16:01. One track. One voiceover.
  2. One session, whole. Warm-up to last rep. Splits in captions.
  3. One race-POV. Next parkrun or 5k. Start, middle, last 400, 20-second debrief.
  4. One FPL-style audio. Last week / this week / next target. Sitting down. Clean sound.
  5. One hub sketch. A free Notion / Google site / Linktree: those four pieces + current PB + his Strava. Steal titles from posts he already wrote. Do not invent a stiffer voice.
Walk into college with those five things and the course is no longer abstract. It is last month’s work, with better cameras.

The only line you need

I’m not picking media instead of running. I’m picking the course where the running is the media — and the folder is the start of the platform.

Ask the teacher on day one

Can my set briefs be answered through youth running, and can Unit 8 be a planned season of race and session films plus a hub? If yes, this pack is the plan. If they lock every brief to horror films or adverts, keep the running folder anyway and use Unit 8 as the open door.
