04–05 · The folder

Units 1–7

Learn-the-tools units first. Pass / fail. Use them to get the channel standing.

Unit 1 · Skills. Film one easy run and one session on the same day. Test: phone vs college camera, mic on the move, Premiere cuts, captions for splits. Hand in the two clips + a page of what broke.
Unit 2 · Research. Pull apart two films: one Nick Bester race-POV, one Olympic training-block (Young / Sesemann / Heymans). Shot list. Who it is for. Then a one-page treatment for your version.
Unit 3 · Professional practice. Build the one-page media kit. How athlete-creators work with brands. Under-18 rules (parent on the email). A fake “if a local shop asked tomorrow” reply. Card-show / eBay brain: listing, value, showing up.
Unit 4 · Contextual. Short video essay: how young runners get shown vs how adults get shown. Parkrun vs elite-wave. Football-to-running switch as the case study — three minutes, not a memoir.

5 · Audio

08 · Before September

Films you can make now

  • One race-POV. Next parkrun or 5k. Start, middle, last 400, 20-second debrief.
