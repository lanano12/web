03 · Approach

        
From first call to running in production.

        
No six-month discovery decks. Working software early, real numbers throughout, and a clean handover at the end.

        

          
WEEK 1–2
Diagnose
We read the code, the dashboards and the incident log. You get a candid map of risks and the highest-leverage move.

          
WEEK 3–5
Prove
A thin, real slice in production — measured against the baseline we agreed. Evidence before commitment.

          
ITERATE
Scale
Pair with your engineers to widen the change safely, behind flags, with tests and observability.

          
HANDOVER
Own it
Docs, runbooks and a team that understands every decision. We leave — the capability stays.

05 · FAQ

        
Questions we often hear.

        

          
Do you work alongside our existing team?
Yes. We embed with your engineers, pair on the work and review together. The goal is that your team owns the result and can carry on without us.

          
Who owns the code and IP?
You do. Everything lives in your repositories and your cloud accounts from day one. No proprietary frameworks, no lock-in.

          
How does an engagement start?
Usually with a short, fixed-scope diagnostic of one to two weeks. You get a candid assessment and a recommended next step, and you decide whether to continue.

          
We’ve never used Rust. Can you still help?
Yes. We help you choose a sensible first component, set up the build, testing and interop with your existing C or C++, and coach your engineers as they go.

          
Can you help us prepare for the EU Cyber Resilience Act?
Yes, on the engineering side: gap analysis, threat modelling, SBOMs, vulnerability handling and secure-development evidence. For legal interpretation we work alongside your legal advisers.

          
Where are you based?
In the UK and remote-first. We are happy to be on site when it genuinely helps, for workshops, kick-offs or critical moments.
