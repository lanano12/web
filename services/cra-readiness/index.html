What we do

Engineering gap review

Map the product, its lifecycle and existing engineering artefacts to the applicable requirements, alongside your regulatory advisers.

Product risk assessment

Record the attack surface, security decisions and the verification needed to support them.

Reporting readiness

Establish ownership and a rehearsed workflow for vulnerability and incident reporting.

Release inventories

Generate machine-readable SBOMs and connect them to specific product versions and build records.

Support & updates

Document the support period, update mechanism and vulnerability-handling process for each product.

Technical evidence

Connect risks, implementation, tests and release records into a maintainable documentation set.

What you get

  • Prioritised engineering readiness plan
  • Product risk assessment and evidence map
  • SBOM and release-record workflow
  • Vulnerability-handling process and technical documentation

How an engagement runs

  1. Understand. Review the product, code and constraints, then agree the scope and acceptance criteria.
  2. Implement and verify. Make reviewable changes and test them against the agreed behaviour and risks.
  3. Hand over. Leave the code, build instructions, evidence and decisions in your team’s hands.
