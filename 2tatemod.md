## Impressionist artists on display at Tate Modern

### Claude Monet

* **Work:** *Water‑Lilies (Nymphéas)*, 1907.
* **Status / where on Tate’s site:** Listed as a free **display: “Claude Monet”** at Tate Modern (the display page features the painting). The page does **not** specify a room number; Tate’s free collection displays are spread across the **Natalie Bell Building** (the original Boiler House) on **Levels 2 & 4**. Use the display page for the latest placement on the day. ([Tate][1])

### Edgar Degas

* **Work:** *Little Dancer Aged Fourteen*, 1880–81, cast c.1922.
* **Location:** On display at **Tate Modern** as part of the **Media Networks** collection display → **Natalie Bell Building, Level 4 (East)**. ([Tate][2])

### Camille Pissarro

* **Work:** *Portrait of Félix Pissarro*, 1881.
* **Location:** On display at **Tate Modern** as part of **Media Networks** → **Natalie Bell Building, Level 4 (East)**. ([Tate][3])

> **Photos / URLs:** The titles above are linked on the Tate site; click the citations to open Tate’s pages with images of the works (and the Monet display page). The thumbnail at the top is from Tate’s Monet display page. ([Tate][1])

## Helpful floor context (from Tate’s site)

Tate Modern’s free collection displays are organised across these signed areas in the **Natalie Bell Building**:

* **Level 2 East – “In the Studio.”** ([Tate][4])
* **Level 2 West – “Artist and Society.”** ([Tate][5])
* **Level 4 East – “Media Networks.”** (where the Degas and Camille Pissarro works above are placed). ([Tate][6])
* **Level 4 West – “Materials and Objects.”** ([Tate][7])

Use the lift/escalators from the Turbine Hall, then follow these section names on the wayfinding signs.

---

## Notes on other Impressionists in Tate’s collection

These are in Tate’s holdings but **not** currently showing at Tate Modern per their artwork pages:

* **Alfred Sisley**, *The Small Meadows in Spring, By* c.1881 – **Not on display**. ([Tate][8])
* **Berthe Morisot**, *Girl on a Divan*, c.1885 – currently noted as **On loan (National Gallery Bicentenary)** on Tate’s site. ([Tate][9])

### Quick checklist for your visit

* **Monet:** open the **Claude Monet display** page before you go for the freshest placement (Tate rotates hangings; the page is the authority). ([Tate][1])
* **Degas & Camille Pissarro:** head to **Media Networks** on **Level 4 (East)** in the **Natalie Bell Building**; look for the Degas *Little Dancer* and Pissarro *Portrait of Félix Pissarro* in that section. ([Tate][2])
