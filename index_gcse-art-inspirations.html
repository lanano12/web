Drawing & mark‑making

Expressive line, tone, and gesture.

Egon Schiele

Angular figure drawings; charged contour and negative space.

Master of precise line and observation.

Brushwork, palette and structure.

Vincent van Gogh

Directional brushwork, complementary colour, emotive landscapes.

Henri Matisse

Fauvist colour; bold shape and rhythm.

Paul Cézanne

Planes of colour; structure under surface.

Kurt Schwitters

Reclining figures; pierced forms; monumental public works.

Barbara Hepworth

Atmospheric light and motion; industrial age vs. nature.

John Constable

Observed skies; English rural scenes and rivers.

Caspar David Friedrich

Romantic mood; the sublime and solitary figures.
